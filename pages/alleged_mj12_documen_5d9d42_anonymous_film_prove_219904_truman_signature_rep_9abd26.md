## Introduction

The Truman signature on the disputed MJ-12 memorandum became one of the sharpest tests of the document because critics identified an apparently matching signature on an unquestionably real Truman letter. The questioned memorandum, dated 24 September 1947 and addressed to Secretary of Defense James [Forrestal]({{ 'forrestal/' | relative_url }}), purports to authorise “Operation Majestic Twelve”. Investigators later compared its presidential signature with one on an authentic 1 October 1947 Truman letter to scientist Vannevar Bush. Critics argued that the similarities extended beyond Truman's normal handwriting to accidental details, making photographic transplantation a more plausible explanation than two independent signings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://priory-of-sion.com/biblios/links/nickell_fischer.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: priory-of-sion.com">[priory-of-sion.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">priory-of-sion.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The crashed-saucer forgeriesby Joe Nickell and John F. Even an examiner cited by prominent MJ-12 defender [Stanton Friedman]({{ 'friedman-s-case/' | relative_url }}) reportedly regarded the questioned signature as a reproduction. ## The comparison that raised the copying allegation

The critical comparison concerns two documents separated by only a week. The MJ-12 memorandum is dated 24 September 1947 and purports to be Truman's instruction to Forrestal. The comparison document is an authentic Truman communication to Vannevar Bush dated 1 October 1947. Sceptical investigators Philip J. Klass, Joe Nickell and John F. Fischer argued that the second supplied the “donor” signature from which the first could have been constructed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://groups.google.com/g/alt.ufo.reports/c/80LgbKV7gPU" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: groups.google.com">[Google Groups]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">groups.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Groups Exciting News! Just Released! Disclosure!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google GroupsExciting News! Just Released! Disclosure...</span></span></span>

The argument was not simply that Truman's signatures looked similar. That would prove little: any person's genuine signatures naturally share habitual letter forms. Nickell and Fischer reported that the two matched after allowance for enlargement and distortion introduced through repeated copying. Their published comparison described the questioned version as darker and slightly stretched, characteristics they attributed to multiple generations of copying.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://priory-of-sion.com/biblios/links/nickell_fischer.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: priory-of-sion.com">[priory-of-sion.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">priory-of-sion.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The crashed-saucer forgeriesby Joe Nickell and John F. FischerJoe Nickell, former private investigatorMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

More striking was the reported correspondence of incidental features. Klass said the two images reproduced accidental scratch or [stray marks]({{ 'stray-mark/' | relative_url }}) associated with the authentic signature. Accounts of the comparison particularly identify an unusual pen movement near the beginning of the “H” in Harry. Such an incidental feature matters because two independently written signatures can be broadly similar without reproducing the same accidental movement at the same position. Klass consequently characterised the MJ-12 version as a photocopied transplant rather than another occasion on which Truman independently wrote his name.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://groups.google.com/g/alt.ufo.reports/c/80LgbKV7gPU" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: groups.google.com">[Google Groups]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">groups.google.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Groups Exciting News! Just Released! Disclosure!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Google GroupsExciting News! Just Released! Disclosure...</span></span></span>

Another detail concerned the capital “T” in Truman. In the authentic Bush letter, part of Truman's signature reportedly overlapped the closing words of the typed complimentary ending. According to the sceptical reconstruction, somebody extracting the signature would therefore have needed to remove the unwanted surrounding material before inserting it into another document. Questioned-document examiner Peter Tytell was reported as finding evidence consistent with such retouching around this part of the signature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://factorelblog.com/2011/04/17/de-cuando-klass-desenmascaro-el-fraude-mj-12/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: factorelblog.com">[factorelblog.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">factorelblog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">De cuando Klass desenmascaro el fraude MJ-12</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Factor el Blog &#124; El Blog de Alejandro AgostinelliApril 17, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2011</span></span></span>

There was also a scale discrepancy. Klass reported the signature on the MJ-12 image as roughly 3.6 per cent larger than its counterpart on the Bush letter. Rather than treating that difference as evidence against copying, he argued that successive photocopy generations could produce small enlargements. His reconstruction attributed the difference to approximately three copying stages.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://factorelblog.com/2011/04/17/de-cuando-klass-desenmascaro-el-fraude-mj-12/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: factorelblog.com">[factorelblog.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">factorelblog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">De cuando Klass desenmascaro el fraude MJ-12</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Factor el Blog &#124; El Blog de Alejandro AgostinelliApril 17, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2011</span></span></span>

This distinction is crucial. The allegation was never that the signature had been laboriously imitated by hand. It was that a **real Truman signature had been mechanically reused**. If that happened, the signature could look entirely authentic at first glance precisely because its source was authentic.

## What document examiners and defenders argued

The strongest sceptical interpretation treats the matching signature as evidence of document construction. Nickell and Fischer stated that the authentic and questioned signatures matched despite the latter's copying distortion and argued that independently executed signatures do not duplicate one another in this fashion. Their conclusion was that the MJ-12 memorandum was spurious.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://priory-of-sion.com/biblios/links/nickell_fischer.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: priory-of-sion.com">[priory-of-sion.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">priory-of-sion.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The crashed-saucer forgeriesby Joe Nickell and John F. FischerJoe Nickell, former private investigatorMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

Tytell's reported examination pushed the argument towards a specific mechanism. Klass recounted that Tytell regarded the image as a classic transplanted signature and considered the altered-looking portion of the “T” particularly significant. That interpretation fits a straightforward fabrication sequence: obtain an authentic Truman document, isolate its signature while masking adjacent material, place the resulting image on newly prepared text and photograph or photocopy the composite until the assembly marks disappear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://factorelblog.com/2011/04/17/de-cuando-klass-desenmascaro-el-fraude-mj-12/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: factorelblog.com">[factorelblog.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">factorelblog.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">De cuando Klass desenmascaro el fraude MJ-12</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Factor el Blog &#124; El Blog de Alejandro AgostinelliApril 17, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2011</span></span></span>

The later work cited by MJ-12 defenders did not necessarily establish that the questioned image represented fresh ink on the disputed memorandum. Friedman reported that questioned-document examiner James A. Black considered the signature “most likely a reproduction”, basing that assessment on properties visible in the image such as the homogeneous appearance of the line and absence of normal feathering at stroke endings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://medium.com/stanton-friedmans-articles/update-on-operation-majestic-12-documents-ed2df58fd8a1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: medium.com">[Medium]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">medium.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UPDATE ON OPERATION MAJESTIC 12 DOCUMENTS &#124; by Richard Geldreich, Jr. &#124; Physicist Stanton Friedman’s Articles &#124; MediumJune 28, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 28, 2024</span></span></span>

That produced a more subtle defence than simply denying reproduction. Friedman argued that **reproduction does not by itself establish forgery**. An authentic Truman-Forrestal memorandum might have existed as an original signed document, he reasoned, while the image eventually leaked to researchers could derive from a reproduced government copy rather than that original. In other words, the proposition “this is an image of a copied signature” and the proposition “a hoaxer copied this signature from the Bush letter” are not logically identical.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://medium.com/stanton-friedmans-articles/update-on-operation-majestic-12-documents-ed2df58fd8a1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: medium.com">[Medium]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">medium.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UPDATE ON OPERATION MAJESTIC 12 DOCUMENTS &#124; by Richard Geldreich, Jr. &#124; Physicist Stanton Friedman’s Articles &#124; MediumJune 28, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 28, 2024</span></span></span>

Friedman also disputed descriptions of the two signatures as simply “exact”. He maintained that measurements of their component parts did not yield uniform ratios and resisted treating visual resemblance alone as decisive evidence of transplantation. His broader position was that investigators had only reproductions and therefore could not reconstruct the complete copying history with certainty.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://medium.com/stanton-friedmans-articles/update-on-operation-majestic-12-documents-ed2df58fd8a1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: medium.com">[Medium]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">medium.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UPDATE ON OPERATION MAJESTIC 12 DOCUMENTS &#124; by Richard Geldreich, Jr. &#124; Physicist Stanton Friedman’s Articles &#124; MediumJune 28, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 28, 2024</span></span></span>

The difficulty for that defence is that the sceptical case depends on more than the generic fact of reproduction. A normal copy of an authentic Truman-Forrestal memorandum could certainly reproduce whatever signature had originally been on it. What needs explaining is why that signature image appears to correspond so closely to the signature on a **different, authentic Truman document**, including reportedly incidental features. If the correspondence is indeed the result of a common image source, ordinary photocopying of two separately signed originals cannot account for it. The defence instead requires some additional copying or signature-reuse process to have occurred within an otherwise authentic documentary history.

Friedman explicitly allowed that possibility. In a later defence he accepted Black's conclusion that the signature had been appended and suggested that a government or intelligence organisation itself could have copied a signature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theufochronicles.com/2014/09/mj-12-stanton-friedman-fires-back.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theufochronicles.com">[The UFO Chronicles]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theufochronicles.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MJ-12: Stanton Friedman Fires Back; The Disputation with Kevin Randle Continues...</span></span></span> That preserves a logical route to authenticity, but it changes the evidential question. The signature can no longer function straightforwardly as proof that Truman personally signed the particular sheet represented in the MJ-12 photograph.

## Why the missing original prevents a decisive test

This dispute is unusually dependent on provenance because researchers do not possess the alleged original Truman-Forrestal memorandum. The document reached the MJ-12 investigators as an image on the anonymously supplied 35 mm film associated with the original MJ-12 papers. Friedman himself described the Truman-Forrestal memorandum as one of the core documents connected with that material.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/update-on-operation-majestic-12-documents/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault Update on Operation Majestic 12 Documents</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultUpdate on Operation Majestic 12 Documents - The Black Vault Case FilesJanuary 17, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 17, 2024</span></span></span>

A photographic image collapses several potentially different physical objects into one visible result. It cannot, by itself, establish whether Truman's ink was actually placed on the alleged 24 September memorandum; whether a genuine signed memorandum was copied and the original subsequently lost; whether an authentic signature was mechanically inserted into a fabricated page; or how many photographic or photocopy generations intervened before the image reached the researchers.

An original could permit materially stronger tests. Examiners could ask whether the signature consisted of ink physically deposited on the same sheet as the typewriting; examine stroke edges and ink behaviour at microscopic level; determine whether corrections, paste-up boundaries or other physical alterations existed; study paper, [watermark]({{ 'watermark/' | relative_url }}) and typewriter impressions; and compare those findings with unquestionably authentic Truman [White House]({{ 'white-house/' | relative_url }}) material. With only a photograph of a photographically transmitted document, some of those distinctions are permanently blurred.

The institutional record compounds that evidential weakness. The US National Archives reports extensive searches of [Air Force]({{ 'air-force/' | relative_url }}), Joint Chiefs of Staff and National Security Council holdings, while the Truman and Eisenhower presidential libraries also searched for references or copies of the MJ-12 documents. Those searches did not uncover an archival Truman-Forrestal original or an authenticated government file establishing its chain of [custody]({{ 'custody/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National Archives...</span></span></span> The FBI separately records that the MJ-12 material referred to it in 1988 was determined to be fake after consultation with the Air Force.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://vault.fbi.gov/unexplained-phenomenon//search" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vault.fbi.gov">[FBI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vault.fbi.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">— Federal Bureau of Investigation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">— Federal Bureau of Investigation</span></span></span>

Those government findings do not constitute a forensic examination of an original Truman-Forrestal sheet — no such sheet was available — but they matter because the alternative explanation proposed by defenders requires an undocumented authentic original or official copying process for which no corresponding archival trail has emerged.

## What the signature can and cannot establish

The signature dispute is strongest when framed narrowly. There is substantial published evidence that the Truman signature image on the MJ-12 memorandum was mechanically reproduced, and sceptical examiners argued that its source can be identified as Truman's authentic 1 October 1947 signature to Vannevar Bush. The reported correspondence of accidental details is considerably more probative than the mere fact that two Truman signatures resemble one another.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://priory-of-sion.com/biblios/links/nickell_fischer.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: priory-of-sion.com">[priory-of-sion.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">priory-of-sion.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The crashed-saucer forgeriesby Joe Nickell and John F. FischerJoe Nickell, former private investigatorMarch 26, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 26, 2026</span></span></span>

Defenders nevertheless identify a genuine limitation in what can be inferred from reproduction alone. A photocopied signature is not automatically a forged signature: government files routinely contain copies rather than wet-ink originals. Friedman therefore argued that an authentic original could once have existed and that the surviving MJ-12 image might descend from a reproduced copy. Even the examiner James Black, as quoted by Friedman, reached a reproduction opinion rather than treating reproduction itself as proof that the underlying memorandum was fabricated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://medium.com/stanton-friedmans-articles/update-on-operation-majestic-12-documents-ed2df58fd8a1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: medium.com">[Medium]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">medium.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UPDATE ON OPERATION MAJESTIC 12 DOCUMENTS &#124; by Richard Geldreich, Jr. &#124; Physicist Stanton Friedman’s Articles &#124; MediumJune 28, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 28, 2024</span></span></span>

The critical distinction is therefore between **reproduction** and **source identification**. If all that could be shown were that the MJ-12 signature was photographically reproduced, the authenticity question would remain substantially open. If, however, the image can reliably be demonstrated to derive from the particular signature on Truman's later Bush letter — complete with its incidental marks and alterations associated with removing neighbouring material — the evidence becomes much harder to reconcile with an independently signed 24 September memorandum.

That is why the Truman signature remains such an important piece of the [anonymous-film]({{ 'anonymous-film/' | relative_url }}) provenance mystery. It does not identify whoever prepared or mailed the 35 mm film, and it cannot reveal every stage through which the photographed document passed. But it supplies a testable relationship between the anonymous MJ-12 image and a known authentic document. On the surviving evidence, the copying allegation has substantial forensic support, while the principal defence depends on a possible but undocumented intermediate history in which an authentic memorandum existed and its signature was reproduced or appended for some legitimate purpose. Without the physical original or an authenticated archival copy, that last possibility cannot be tested directly — but neither can the photographed signature serve as independent evidence that Truman personally signed the disputed MJ-12 memorandum.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://priory-of-sion.com/biblios/links/nickell_fischer.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: priory-of-sion.com">[priory-of-sion.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">priory-of-sion.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The crashed-saucer forgeriesby Joe Nickell and John F.
