## Introduction

## Why 24 September 1947 looks historically plausible

The strongest genuine chronological fact supporting the surface plausibility of the story is Truman's appointment diary. The Truman Library records that at **11:30 a.m. on 24 September 1947**, the president met jointly with **James Forrestal and Vannevar Bush**, with the meeting arranged at Forrestal's suggestion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.trumanlibrary.gov/calendar/search?body_value_op=word&amp;comment_k=&amp;page=75" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trumanlibrary.gov">[Truman Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trumanlibrary.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Truman Library Daily Appointments of Harry S. TrumanSeptember 23, 1947...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 23, 1947</span></span></span>

That is an unusually close match to the cast of the alleged MJ-12 foundation story. The 1952 briefing says Truman acted on the recommendation of Forrestal and Bush, while the supposed Truman memo opens by referring to a “recent conversation”. The authentic diary establishes that all three men were indeed together on the day assigned to the alleged authorisation.

It does **not**, however, establish what they discussed. The calendar entry supplies no MJ-12 reference, UFO subject or Roswell connection. Nor does it establish whether the alleged memorandum was supposedly written before or after the 11:30 meeting. The coincidence therefore strengthens the documents' historical realism without authenticating their contents.

The broader institutional timing is equally notable. Forrestal had become the United States' **first Secretary of Defense only on 17 September**, seven days before the alleged MJ-12 order. Truman had ordered his swearing-in brought forward because the newly created national-security structure needed to become operational quickly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.defense.gov/Multimedia/Biographies/Article-View/Article/571293/james-v-forrestal/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.defense.gov">[Defense Historical Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Historical Office James V. The **Central Intelligence Agency formally came into existence on 18 September**, replacing the Central Intelligence Group under the National Security Act of 1947. Contemporary records show Director of Central Intelligence Roscoe [Hillenkoetter]({{ 'hillenkoetter/' | relative_url }}) reorganising intelligence arrangements under the new statutory framework that same day.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/intelligence/cia" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on archives.gov.</span></span></span>

Finally, the **first meeting of the National Security Council took place on 26 September**, only two days after the alleged order. Truman presided; Forrestal and Hillenkoetter were among those present.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.state.gov//historicaldocuments/frus1945-50Intel/d225fn1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.state.gov">[Office of the Historian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.state.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Office of the Historian Historical Documents</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of the Historian Historical Documents</span></span></span>

An alleged covert committee dated to that week can therefore appear deeply embedded in real institutional change even without leaving independent evidence of its own.

## The post-Roswell chronology

The alleged authorisation is commonly described as occurring “immediately after Roswell”, but the interval is more precisely about eleven weeks. The Roswell Army Air Field announced recovery of a “flying disc” on **8 July 1947**; the alleged Truman order is dated **24 September**. A later GAO review confirmed the existence of two contemporary government records connected with the July episode: a 509th Bomb Group/Roswell Army Air Field history and an FBI teletype of 8 July. The FBI message reported an object resembling a high-altitude balloon with a radar reflector.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://irp.fas.org/gao/nsi95187.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: irp.fas.org">[Intelligence Resource Program]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">irp.fas.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Intelligence Resource Program GAO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Intelligence Resource Program GAO</span></span></span>

Within the MJ-12 narrative, those intervening weeks are supposed to have been a period of investigation culminating in presidential-level organisation. The 1952 briefing presents the July recovery as the precipitating event and the 24 September action as the administrative response.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ia800500.us.archive.org/35/items/majestic-12-documents-for-majic-eyes-only/Eisenhower%20Briefing%20Document_text.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ia800500.us.archive.org">[Archive.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ia800500.us.archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">SECRET / MAJIC</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">a -uro ALL) varomi Bamir OOctober 30, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 30, 2024</span></span></span>

The authentic federal record does not reproduce that chain. GAO's later search examined records from the Pentagon, FBI, CIA, National Security Council and other agencies and found no contemporary documentation establishing an extraterrestrial recovery or subsequent MJ-12 organisation. It also noted an important complication: some Roswell Army Air Field administrative records and outgoing messages for the relevant era had been destroyed without adequate documentation of the disposal authority. Missing local records, however, are not equivalent to evidence for a presidential committee. The alleged 24 September action would have operated at a much higher level than Roswell Army Air Field and supposedly involved the presidency, Secretary of Defense, Director of Central Intelligence, senior officers and prominent civilian scientists. Its evidential footprint would therefore be expected across several record systems, not just the files lost at Roswell.

## The date fits better than the surviving paper trail

The most important distinction is between **chronological compatibility** and **documentary corroboration**.

Chronologically, the story is impressively constructed. Forrestal had the right title by 24 September; the CIA and its director existed in their new institutional setting; Bush was an obvious figure for high-level scientific advice; and Truman demonstrably met Forrestal and Bush on the exact day named in the alleged papers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://history.defense.gov/Multimedia/Biographies/Article-View/Article/571293/james-v-forrestal/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.defense.gov">[defense.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Historical Office James V. ## What surviving records would be expected?

Secrecy makes this question more complicated than simply asking whether an executive order appeared in the Federal Register. A genuinely compartmented presidential programme need not have been announced publicly, and Truman used confidential presidential directives in national-security affairs. The lack of a published “Executive Order creating MJ-12” therefore cannot by itself settle the question.

The more telling issue is the absence of **collateral documentation**. If the alleged memorandum accurately describes a continuing presidential operation involving Forrestal, Bush and the Director of Central Intelligence, researchers would reasonably look for some combination of:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* references in Truman's presidential files or the papers of his senior aides;
* Forrestal correspondence, memoranda, appointment records or defence-office files;
* Bush papers reflecting a special presidential scientific assignment;
* CIA or Director of Central Intelligence correspondence concerning membership, briefings or reporting;
* National Security Council indexes, minutes or administrative records;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/273.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[archives.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Source details in endnotes.</span></span></span>
* records establishing later appointments, replacements, clearances, funding or responsibility for the supposed organisation.

</div>

No single missing category would be decisive. Highly sensitive programmes can create deliberately sparse records, euphemistic references or files retained outside normal channels. But the alleged MJ-12 scheme is presented as lasting for years and involving twelve senior figures. The longer and more bureaucratically complex such an organisation became, the harder it would be for every trace to disappear simultaneously from presidential, defence, intelligence and personal archival collections.

The National Archives' search is therefore particularly relevant. Its MJ-12 reference work states that researchers searched records of the Secretary of Defense, Joint Chiefs of Staff, Air Force and related bodies, while inquiries to other national-security organisations produced no confirming material. The Truman and Eisenhower Libraries likewise found no copies or references corresponding to the core MJ-12 documents.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National Archives...</span></span></span>

## What the September 1947 timeline can actually establish

The historical record confirms an intriguing set of circumstances around **24 September 1947**. Truman, Forrestal and Bush were all active at the centre of a rapidly changing national-security system; Forrestal and the CIA had only just assumed their new institutional forms; Truman met Forrestal and Bush together that day; and the first National Security Council meeting followed two days later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.trumanlibrary.gov/calendar/search?body_value_op=word&amp;comment_k=&amp;page=75" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trumanlibrary.gov">[trumanlibrary.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trumanlibrary.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Truman Library Daily Appointments of Harry S. What the record does **not** confirm is the additional step claimed by the MJ-12 papers: that the subject of the 24 September meeting was Roswell, that Bush and Forrestal recommended a twelve-man extraterrestrial recovery committee, or that Truman issued a classified order creating “Operation Majestic Twelve”.

That distinction is the central lesson of the alleged authorisation timeline. The date is not random or obviously impossible; indeed, it is one of the most historically persuasive details in the MJ-12 story. But a plausible date is not the same thing as authenticated governance. After extensive searches of presidential, defence, intelligence and archival holdings, the decisive independent record that would turn the 24 September narrative from an allegation into a documented Truman action has not been found.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gao.gov/products/154832" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gao.gov">[Government Accountability Office]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gao.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Government Accountability Office[Comments on Majestic 12 Material] &#124; U.S. GAO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government Accountability Office[Comments on Majestic 12 Material] &#124; U.S. GAO</span></span></span>
